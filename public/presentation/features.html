Auth

Accounts that stick.

Sign up

Name, @handle (3–24), email, password. Default bio and SVG avatar on create.

Log in

NextAuth credentials (JWT). Banned users blocked at login and middleware.

Protected app

All /app routes require a session. Landing redirects logged-in users home.

Sign out

Available from your own profile. Session cleared cleanly.

Home feed

Posts, people, and pulse.

  • Chronological feed of text, photo, and clip posts
  • Stories rail across the top — jump to create your own
  • Like, follow, view counts, and type labels on every card
  • Equipped frames, badges, and titles render on authors
  • Unique views after ~800ms award the author Sparks

Stories

Twenty-four hours. Then gone.

Create

Image upload or URL plus optional caption. Deep-link from the home rail.

View

Full-screen overlay from the rail. Caption on top. Tap to close.

Expiry

Stories expire after 24 hours — ephemeral by design.

Home-native

Lives on the Home rail; /stories routes back to the feed.

For You

Vertical clips that pay.

  • TikTok-style full-bleed clip player — muted, looped, inline
  • Swipe, scroll wheel, or arrow / j / k to move between clips
  • Side actions: follow, like, and position counter
  • Unique view after ~600ms on focus → Sparks for the creator
  • Tap @handle to open a public profile

Auction House

Bid Sparks. Win custom emotes.

Live bidding

Min bid = start or current+1. You can’t raise while you’re already leading.

Escrow

Bid held in ledger. Outbid refunds the previous leader. Cancel refunds too.

Win → chat

Highest bidder gets an OwnedEmote usable as a DM sticker (image or glyph).

Inventory

Your emotes + recent ended/cancelled results. Expired auctions settle on load.

Admin

Infrastructure

Cloud-native. Data-safe.

  • Next.js 15 on Vercel
  • Accounts, posts, chats on Neon Postgres (Prisma)
  • Photos & videos on Vercel Blob (local /uploads in dev)
  • Auth with NextAuth credentials + JWT
  • Deploys update code — never wipe user data
